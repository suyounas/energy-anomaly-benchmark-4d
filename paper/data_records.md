# Data Records
### (Structure follows Gillioz et al., Sci Data 12, 168 (2025), Table 3)

All raw time series are re-downloadable from their public sources via the
acquisition scripts in `code/`; they are not redistributed in this
repository (size and licensing). Derived results tables and figures are
included in `results/`.

| Study | Series | Source & access | Resolution | Rows used | Period | Ground-truth events (verified) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | WTI crude oil spot (DCOILWTICO) | FRED, direct CSV, no key | daily | ~1.5k | 2018–2023 | Texas Freeze (Feb 2021); Russia–Ukraine (Feb–Mar 2022) |
| 1 | Henry Hub natural gas (DHHNGSP) | FRED, direct CSV, no key | daily | ~1.5k | 2019–2023 | same two events |
| 2 | FR national load; 5 countries used of 25 | Gillioz et al., Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.13378476 (`loads_YYYY_1.csv`, 4,097 buses → national aggregate) | hourly | 43,656/country | 2016–2020 (364-day years; one 7-day join gap, verified) | COVID-19 (−20.7% vs real ~15–20% ✓); nuclear crisis (attenuated, per documented annual-flow constraint) |
| 2 | FR cross-border line flows, 62 lines → 28 crossings | same source (`lines_YYYY_1.csv`, 8,375 lines; topology `europe_network.json`) | hourly | 26,184 | 2016–2018 | nuclear crisis 2016-17 (flow-side) |
| 3 | GB national demand (ND) | NESO Data Portal via CKAN API (`demanddata_YYYY`) | half-hourly (48 settlement periods/day) | 140,240 (complete; 16-row DST accounting verified) | 2016–2023 | COVID lockdown (−19.1% YoY ✓); Dec 2022 cold snap (+6.4% YoY, weak) |
| 4 | ERCOT demand, adjusted (EIA-930) | EIA Hourly Grid Monitor six-month balance files, direct CSV, stream-filtered to BA=ERCO | hourly | 43,824 (5 complete years, zero gaps) | 2019–2023 | Texas Freeze (+30.9% YoY recorded, load-shed caveat); Winter Storm Elliott (+49.4% YoY) |

**Derived records in this repository** (`results/`): per-study
precision/recall/F1 tables under both evaluation protocols
(`*_phase3_results.csv` / `final_corrected_results.csv`), per-event
recall and unlabelled-window diagnostics (Studies 3–4), and all
publication figures (`figures_unified/`, per-study `figures/`).
Intermediate artefacts regenerated by the pipeline (features, splits,
aggregated national/crossing series) are stored on the user's working
Drive, not in the repository; every one is reproducible from the
numbered scripts in `code/`.
